Melonia, officially known as the Republic of Melonia, is a defunct online Minecraft nation. Melonia is considered to be the predecessor to the Chiefdom of Osolia as the Chiefdom's formation came as a result of shutting down Melonia. Before the server shutdown, it was a semi-presidential constitutional republic that was founded as a representative city-state as the City of Melonia.
