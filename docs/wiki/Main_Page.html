From Osolia Wiki

Welcome to the Osolia Wiki!

This wiki is operated by the Osolia Historical Society, an agency independent of the Chiefdom of Osolia government that seeks to protect and preserve the history of the Chiefdom of Osolia.

Only Osolian citizens are allowed to edit this wiki. For more information, please contact Michael Webb (TheRandomMelon).

Articles for new citizens to read

Here are some recommended articles for new citizens to read, along with a short explainer.

Name Description
Chief of Osolia The head/leader of the Chiefdom of Osolia. Learn about what they specifically do and the position's history here.
Speaker of Osolia The leader of the legislative branch (the Assembly) of the Chiefdom of Osolia. Learn about what they specifically do and the position's history here.
Chiefdom of Osolia An overview of the country as a whole.
Department of Infrastructure The department responsible for infrastructure across Osolia.
Chief's Council A general article discussing what the Chief's Council is.
Osolian Assembly The legislative branch of the Chiefdom of Osolia. All citizens are members of the Assembly. Learn more about its history and its specific functions here.
Law in Osolia This article describes how a law is created, passed, enforced and amended.
Elections in Osolia This article describes how and when elections are ran in Osolia.
