FlynneSkye, usually referred to by her real-life name, Sharna, or an abbreviated form of her username, Flynne, is the current and first ever Governor of Sahosam. She is the owner of The Inconvience Store located in Sahosam. She was invited to the server by TheGoldenPud, who is her boyfriend.

Tenure as Governor of Sahosam (August 17, 2024 - present)

Since taking the reins as the first Governor of Sahosam on August 17, 2024, Flynne has not put much into action due to being busy in her personal life.

However, she has outlined her plans for Sahosam in a document titled "Sahosam Design Ideas", in which she proposes a partial reconstruction and expansion to Sahosam. This includes expanding the Mellen River, a riverbank terrace redesign for the edges around the river, a medieval redesign for the bridges in Sahosam, and a general medieval design throughout all publicly owned areas.

Little feedback has been given on this proposal thus far, and none of its proposals have moved forward so far, as of September 10, 2024.
