The Sahosam Central Railway Station is a railway station in Sahosam owned and operated by Oslink. It was designed and built by Hydrogen, who founded and currently owns Oslink.

Architecture

The station was constructed with polished granite, bricks, stone bricks, stone, and glass. The building is open on all sides, making access easier.

Network

Sahosam Central currently provides services to Sahosam Hills, the layout of which has been subject to controversy and several revisions. More lines are planned to be added, with the aim of connecting nearby locations with railway lines.

The station is also home to to the main Sahosam Nether portal.
