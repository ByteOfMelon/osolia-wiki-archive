The DJ Hour is an interactive radio program that has been hosted on an irregular basis on Osolia Public Radio since January 8, 2024. The show was created by and is hosted by Michael Webb, with tz_scion serving as producer on most episodes.

Episodes

Main article: List of The DJ Hour episodes
Season Episodes First Aired Last Aired
1 10 January 8, 2024 June 16, 2024
2 9 July 28, 2024 August 29, 2025

Each episode of The DJ Hour consists of host Michael Webb collecting song requests to play on-air, providing some commentary on each song selection before and after playing the song. About halfway through each show, Webb will take a break from listener-requested songs to play a special song created by an Osolian citizen, typically by tz_scion specifically. Webb will also usually play his own song choice at the very beginning and end of each DJ Hour episode.

Trivia

  • The DJ Hour was the first ever show to air on Osolia Public Radio (OPR) on January 8, 2024, and is still OPR's most popular show.
  • Quite a few songs by tz_scion were debuted first live on The DJ Hour, such as "Imposing Premonition (Remaster)", "Nature's Home", and "Keep Going Onward".
  • Additionally, Chromaforge, a collaborative music group between tz_scion and Michael Webb, debuted their first song, "Uncanny Valley" first live on The DJ Hour.
  • The DJ Hour was the last show to be broadcast on OPR before its OPR 2.0 project was released.
